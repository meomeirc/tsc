place your songs inside this folder. TSC will automatically scan
this folder and its subfolders when opening Song Select.

organize songs into packs, with each song in its own folder:

songs/
  My Pack/
    My Song/
      music.opus
      song.json
      ez.tsc
      nm.tsc
    My Other Song/
      song.opus
      song.json
      charts/
        easy.ares
        normal.ares
        hard.ares

you can also put bundles inside this folder:

songs/
  My Pack/
    My Song.tsts
    My Song.zip

imported bundles are automatically removed after extraction and validation,
otherwise kept as the original files

i do recommend organizing your charts for the editor in this way,
it gives a clean way to see all your charts in the program.

you can make a new chart from the editor and save it into for example 'songs/My Pack/My Song/'

do not put unrelated songs inside the same song folder (eg. one song folder having chart files for multiple songs).
you can create as many pack folders and song folders as needed

after adding songs, reopen Song Select to see them
