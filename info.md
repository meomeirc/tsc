# TSC

This program is under active development. For feedback or bug reports, ping me (@phigos).

## Export a chart for ReSONANCE

1. Create a chart.
2. Choose a template. Both are supported; `.tsc` is the text representation of the ARES format.
3. Edit your chart.
4. Choose **Export as TSTS** from the Info menu.
5. Upload the `.tsts` bundle to ReSONANCE.
